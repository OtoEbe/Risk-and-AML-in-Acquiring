How the final reports behave

Each participant gets a standalone Final Training Report, one HTML file mirroring an LMS completion record: score, pass or fail status against your threshold, rounds completed, verification ID, the per-round table, and your remarks and signature block. It opens in any browser, prints to PDF in one click, and needs no LMS or licence. The cohort zip bundles every report; the CSV covers gradebook imports. Sending opens a prefilled email in your own mail client; attach the downloaded report before it goes.
